Here is a battle-tested strategy tailored for Gen Z and college creators.

### Part 1: 3 Hook Ideas (First 3 Seconds)

*Note: For college students, the highest-converting hooks combine **relatability (low effort/high reward)** with **a direct call-out of their current failure (the 200-view jail).***

1. **The “Brutal Reality Check” (Negative Bias Hook)**
   * **Visual:** You sitting in a lecture hall or dorm, looking directly at the camera with an exhausted expression, pointing at a phone screen showing "214 views."
   * **Audio:** *"Stop spending two hours editing a TikTok just for it to die at 200 views."*
   * **On-Screen Text:** *“Why your videos get 200 views…”*

2. **The “Unfair Advantage” (Curiosity Loop)**
   * **Visual:** Close-up of you typing frantically on your laptop in class, switching quickly to your phone showing a viral video notification.
   * **Audio:** *"This AI tool is literally making college kids go viral during their 8 AM lectures."*
   * **On-Screen Text:** *“The AI hack college creators are gatekeeping 🤫”*

3. **The Pattern Interrupt (Visual Contrast)**
   * **Visual:** Start by throwing a textbook off your desk or slamming your phone down, then lean in close to the lens.
   * **Audio:** *"Your content isn't bad—your first 3 seconds just suck."*
   * **On-Screen Text:** *“The real reason you have no views 📉”*

* **Target Pace:** ~70 words. Fast, dynamic delivery with quick jump-cuts every 1.5–2 seconds.
* **Music:** Low-fi trending phonk or subtle upbeat bounce (low volume under voiceover).

### Viral Strategist Tips for Filming This:
* **Don't make it look like an ad:** Shoot this on a phone in a messy dorm or campus library, **not** a sterile studio. High production value looks like an ad; raw UGC (User-Generated Content) builds trust.
* **Auto-captions are mandatory:** 70% of viewers watch without sound. Use dynamic, highlighted captions (CapCut style) with emojis.
